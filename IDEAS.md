# EntangleIT app ideas — OS citizenship verdict (F15)

Seven app ideas competed for system status. Most are store apps, not OS
surface. This decision is final unless relitigated; each promotion below
becomes its own F-item when built.

## Citizens (system surface)

- **GigsFeed** — TikTok-style paid micro-gigs with one-tap approval.
  Lives as the bounty board in the launcher (→ F12). Reference feed for
  the BSVBounties/agentpay escrow flow (claim → submit → approve, earnings
  as labeled basket entries per F4).
- **NightShift** — always-on agent ops as a system service for standing
  orders with per-cycle escrow (→ F13). Bound to agent sub-wallet budgets
  (F9); schedules managed in shell.

## Onboarding helper

- **MemeFaucet** — trial faucet dealing starter sats to first-run wallets
  (capped, one-per-wallet). Not built: needs a funded, rate-limited faucet
  service first; becomes its own F-item when the treasury side exists.

## Share target

- **AskAnything** — sats-attached Q&A as a share-sheet destination. The
  F7 share path (`bsv share`, panel Share section, Nautilus target) is the
  integration seam; the widget itself is a future F-item.

## Store-only (no system surface)

Linked from `bsv store` once they ship manifests (F1); until then they
live here, not in the catalog:

- **Agent Arena** — store listing only.
- **TinyBets** — store listing only, age-gated out of the catalog
  defaults when listed.
- **BitTok** — store listing only.

## Non-decisions (explicitly out of scope)

- No OS-level token, no built-in exchange, no custodial "bank" features:
  the wallet stays self-custody and policy-gated throughout.
- No preinstalled third-party apps beyond the loopback Runner Demo (dev).
